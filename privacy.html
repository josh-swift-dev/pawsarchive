Our Commitment to Privacy

PawsArchive is designed to help you organize your pets' medical information while keeping you in control of your records.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the PawsArchive app.

1. Information You Enter

You may enter information including pet names, photographs, medical histories, vaccination records, medications, weight measurements, veterinarian information, insurance details, and emergency contacts.

This information is stored locally on your device. PawsArchive does not require you to create an account.

2. Local Data Storage

Your pet profiles, records, and imported attachments are stored in the app's local storage on your device.

PawsArchive does not operate its own cloud storage service or automatically upload your pet records to our servers.

Your device's operating system and backup settings may separately affect how app data is backed up.

3. Camera, Photos, and Documents

With your permission, PawsArchive can access your camera to photograph receipts or other pet-related documents.

You may also select photographs and documents to include in your pet's records. These files are copied into the app's local storage.

You control which photographs and documents you choose to add.

4. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, PawsArchive can schedule local reminders for vaccinations and other pet-care tasks.

You can manage notification permissions through your device's Settings.

5. Purchases

PawsArchive offers an optional, one-time purchase called PawsArchive Pro.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not directly receive or store your payment-card information.

Apple's handling of payment and account information is governed by Apple's applicable privacy policies.

6. Backups and Sharing

You can export backups and medical reports using the app's sharing features.

Once you export or share a file, its storage and handling depend on the destination you select, such as Files, email, or a third-party cloud storage provider.

Backups may contain sensitive information, including contact details and medical records. Store and share them securely.

7. Advertising and Analytics

PawsArchive does not include third-party advertising or third-party analytics in the version covered by this policy.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app and its data.

Deleting the app may permanently remove its locally stored records. Export a backup before uninstalling or changing devices.

Files you previously exported must be deleted separately from wherever you chose to save them.

9. Children's Privacy

PawsArchive is a pet-record management application and is not specifically directed toward children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as PawsArchive develops. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
