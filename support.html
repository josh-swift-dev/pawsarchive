Welcome to Support

PawsArchive helps you organize your pets' medical records, vaccination history, health reminders, medical information, and important documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PawsArchive free?

Yes. You can create one pet profile for free, including medical records, vaccinations, reminders, and backups.

What is PawsArchive Pro?

PawsArchive Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited pets and premium features, including PDF medical reports and weight charts. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open PawsArchive, navigate to Settings, and select Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account associated with your original purchase.

Where are my pet records stored?

Your records and attachments are stored locally on your device. You can create and export a full backup from the app's Settings.

Can I transfer my records to another iPhone?

Yes. Export a full backup from your original device and import it on your new device. Keep your backup somewhere secure.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check that notifications are enabled for PawsArchive in your iPhone's Settings. Also verify your reminder dates and times.

Can I cancel my Pro purchase?

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. There are no recurring charges to cancel. For refund requests, contact Apple.

Troubleshooting

If something isn't working, try restarting the app and checking for updates. Before reinstalling the app, export a full backup of your records. Deleting the app may remove locally stored data.
